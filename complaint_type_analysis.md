# Complaint Type Analysis

The most abundant complaint type during January and February 2024 was **HEAT/HOT WATER**, with a total of **81,631 complaints**.

The monthly counts were:

- January: 51,320
- February: 30,311
- June: 3,513
- July: 3,376

In comparison, June and July had only **6,889 HEAT/HOT WATER complaints** in total.

This shows a strong seasonal pattern. HEAT/HOT WATER complaints were much more common during the winter months than during the summer months, which is expected because heating and hot water issues are more likely to be reported during colder weather.
